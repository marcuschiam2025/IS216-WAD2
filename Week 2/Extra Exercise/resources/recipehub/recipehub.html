Carbonara

A Roman classic — silky egg-yolk sauce, crispy guanciale, and a cloud of Pecorino Romano.

⏱️25 minTotal time
👥4Servings
⚡EasyDifficulty
🔥620kcal / serve

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g guanciale
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 100g Pecorino Romano
  • 50g Parmesan
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp sea salt

Chef's notes

The only secret is temperature control. Too hot and the eggs scramble; too cool and the sauce won't emulsify. Remove the pan from heat before adding the egg mixture.
